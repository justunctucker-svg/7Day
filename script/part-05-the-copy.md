# PROJECT ECHO 07 — PART 5: "THE COPY"

**Format:** 2D animated short **Runtime:** ~60 seconds
**Dialogue:** Ward only. Ward's voice now comes out of the double's mouth in the room, and at the very end it plays from a radio somewhere else, filtered again.

**Status:** DRAFT for approval. No animatic yet.

---

## The engine of this episode

| Device | How it works |
|---|---|
| **The standoff never breaks** | For half the episode neither man lowers his gun. Every cut returns to the two barrels, and the trembling gets worse. |
| **The audience loses track of who's who** | When the lights die, the only things visible are two identical glowing marks, grappling, circling and crossing. By the time the gun goes off, nobody watching can be sure which Daniel is which. |
| **The siege behind them** | The hatch they came through buckles under the infected: bolts straining, a steady hammering that gets faster with the heartbeat. |
| **One shot, no answer** | One round each, one gunshot, one man left standing. The episode never says which one. |
| **The cycle restarts** | The final minute shows that this has happened before and is about to happen again. The series title itself changes. |

## Why the cliffhanger lands

- **It never says who survived.** The survivor speaks with Ward's voice, which suggests the double. But the audience lost track in the dark, and his eyes are hidden in the red light. Both readings stay alive into Part 6.
- **It reframes the whole series.** Part 1's warning ("don't leave the farmhouse") wasn't a rescue; it's a script, and it's being read to the next subject. Project Echo is a loop.
- **The callback hits hard.** The same opening eye, the same `DAY ONE` stamp, the same 60 bpm heartbeat, but a new person. Viewers who watched from Part 1 will feel it immediately.
- **The title changes.** `PATIENT 07` becoming `PATIENT 08` is the last thing on screen, and it tells the audience the story has just moved to someone new.

## Production notes

1. **Reuse:** the mirror two-shot, the bunker, the monitor wall, the console and headset, the hatch, the revolver and the muzzle-flash frame (Part 4); the arm framing and the eye-opening ECU (Part 1); the radio prop (Part 1); the face-wall eyes (Part 4).
2. **New:** the doubled 07-A / 07-B traces, the buckling hatch, the cascading tube blackout, the two-marks-in-the-dark struggle, the red emergency lighting, the bowing infected, the dial turning to CH 08, Subject 08's bedroom and her eye (green iris).
3. **The clock:** it starts at Part 4's ending tempo (about 140 bpm, doubled). It drops to a single heartbeat after the shot and slows. On the title card it returns to exactly 60 bpm, the clock from Part 1.
4. **The dark sequence is choreography.** The two marks follow paths that cross at least twice before the shot, so the audience genuinely can't track them.
5. **Ward's voice:** the first three lines are dry, in the room (the same chain as Part 4's last line). The last line is filtered as a radio, exactly like Part 1's broadcast.
6. **The shot stays non-graphic:** a white frame, then a body seen still on the floor in red light.
7. **Deliverables match Parts 1–4:** a playable animatic plus 16:9 and 9:16 MP4s.
