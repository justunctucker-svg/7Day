# PROJECT ECHO 07 — PART 1: "THE LAST BROADCAST"

## Production notes

1. **The mark must match.** The puncture mark planted at 00:03–00:05 (#02) and the glowing mark at 00:51–00:56 (#18, #20) are the same shot setup: same arm framing, same position on the forearm, same small size before the glow starts. In the animatic both shots are drawn by the same `drawForearm()` routine from one `MARK` constant, and the **Mark match** button shows the two frames side by side with a crosshair. Layout and final animation should lock the arm framing from #02 and reuse it for #18/#20, changing only the lighting grade and the glow layer.
2. **The first 42 seconds run on picture, SFX and music alone.** The only line before the radio is Daniel's quiet "It'll pass. It always does." Ward's two lines are the only real dialogue.
3. **Day stamps carry the time compression.** Short stamp-style cards only (`DAY ONE`, `DAY 2`, `DAY 3`, `DAY 5`, `DAY 6`, `DAY 8`, `DAY 9`), never full sentences. `DAY ONE` is small and lower-third; the montage stamps slam in on the cut.
4. **Visual rhyme, radio ↔ mark.** The radio's indicator light and the mark's glow share the same teal and the same heartbeat rhythm, so the radio "pulsing" at 00:42 already points at the mark before Ward says it. *(Animatic choice; drop it if it gives the reveal away too early.)*
5. **The knocks are edit points.** Each knock lands on a hard cut; the third knock is the freeze frame. No music under the knocks, only room tone.
