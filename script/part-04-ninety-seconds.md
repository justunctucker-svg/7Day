# PROJECT ECHO 07 — PART 4: "NINETY SECONDS"

**Format:** 2D animated short **Runtime:** ~60 seconds
**Dialogue:** Ward only. Three lines over the walkie (radio treatment plus squelch), then one line **in the room, unfiltered**, for the first time in the series.

Playable animatic: [`animatic/part-04.html`](../animatic/part-04.html) (open in any browser, press Space).

---

## The engine of this episode: keep them on edge

| Device | How it works |
|---|---|
| **A ticking clock** | Ward gives Daniel ninety seconds. Countdown stamps replace the day stamps (`1:30`, `0:52`, `0:17`, `0:03`) and jump forward at each cut, so time visibly runs out. |
| **The heartbeat speeds up** | The series' heartbeat, steady at 60 bpm for three episodes, climbs to about 140 bpm by the end. The mark and the LED pulse faster with it, so the audience feels it before they notice it. |
| **One light, and it's dying** | The flashlight flickers and finally dies in the tunnel. After that the only light is his mark. |
| **Something is always behind him** | Sound from behind that you never quite see, until the muzzle flash shows you everything at once. |
| **Bullets as stakes** | Two rounds left after Part 3. He spends one. The cliffhanger is a standoff with **one round**. |

## Why the cliffhanger lands

- **It rewrites everything the audience thought they knew.** The voice that guided Daniel for four episodes belongs to someone with his face. Was "Ward" ever Ward? Is Daniel the copy, or is he?
- **The monitors make it bigger than one man.** Subjects 08–12 are alive somewhere, so the series has room to grow.
- **One round, a mirror and no answer.** Viewers have to come back to find out who fires, and who is real.
- **It breaks the rules on purpose.** The heartbeat has been steady and Ward has always been on a radio. Both change in this episode, so the audience feels that something has broken.

## Production notes

1. **Reuse:** the cornfield and eyes and the boarded window (Part 2); the closet floor and hole, the walkie, the revolver and cylinder insert, the flashlight and beam, and the muzzle-flash frame (Part 3); the arm framing (Part 1).
2. **New:** the red LED state, the tunnel (dirt walls, roots, tally marks, carved 07s), the face-wall muzzle-flash frame, the hatch and scanner plate, the bunker (beds, the monitor wall with twelve traces), the console and the second Daniel with teal eyes.
3. **Heartbeat ramp:** the shared clock runs at about 60 bpm until the LED turns red, then ramps to about 140 bpm by 00:54, then locks into perfect sync with the second Daniel's.
4. **Ward's last line is dry.** It uses the same voice and pitch as the walkie lines, with no radio filter and a little room reverb, so the audience recognises it as the same person.
5. **Countdown stamps** use the existing stamp style, replacing day stamps for this episode only.
6. **The flash frame stays non-graphic.** Faces in silhouette and teal eyes, held for one frame.
7. **Deliverables match Parts 1–3:** a playable animatic plus 16:9 and 9:16 MP4s.
