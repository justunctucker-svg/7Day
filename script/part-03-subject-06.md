## Why it works as Part 3

- **The gun is earned, not convenient.** Ward's "I left you something" from Part 2 pays off a second time: the folder was the truth, and the gun is the means. The note ("ONLY IF IT COMES UP") makes it Ward's plan all along.
- **It raises the stakes on who Ward is.** The wristband says Subject 06 *is* Ward. So the calm voice on the walkie is someone else, or Ward was never who Daniel thought.
- **The last line lands as a threat.** "Good. Now you're ready." turns the shooting into a test that Daniel just passed for someone else.
- **The heartbeat device escalates.** Two marks pulsing in sync becomes one mark pulsing alone. It's the series' rhythm made into the emotional beat.

## Production notes

1. **Reuse sets.** The closet, floorboards and gap, the eye, the flashlight pool, the walkie and the shelf all come from Part 2. New pieces: the revolver and ammo carton, the paper tag, the loading inserts, Subject 06 (character design), the wristband close-up, the muzzle flash and the aftermath smoke.
2. **One clock.** Subject 06's chest mark, Daniel's arm and the walkie LED share Part 1's heartbeat clock. During the standoff both marks hit on the same frame. After the shot, only Daniel's continues.
3. **The flashlight is the camera's light.** After the drop at 00:01, every shot is lit by a single beam from floor level, so it tilts, swings and comes to rest. No other light until the muzzle flash.
4. **The shot is one frame of white.** A full white frame at the trigger pull, then black. No gore on screen; the aftermath is shown through the smoke and the glow going out.
5. **Ward only.** Three short lines with walkie squelches. The feedback squeal at 00:44 is a sound effect, not Ward.
6. **No day stamp.** It's the same night as Part 2's `DAY 10`, continuous action.
7. **Deliverables match Parts 1 and 2:** a playable animatic plus 16:9 and 9:16 MP4s.
