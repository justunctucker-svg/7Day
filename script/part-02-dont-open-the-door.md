## Why it works as Part 2

- **It answers the knocks without opening the door.** Ward's warning from Part 1 ("don't leave the farmhouse") becomes the rule the whole episode obeys.
- **It explains the mark without a lecture.** One Ward line ("That mark is how they find you"), then the field of eyes pulsing in its rhythm shows it.
- **The farmhouse becomes a trap.** The closet set up in Part 1 is the payoff, and Subject 06 under the floor turns the safe house into the threat.
- **It sets up Part 3:** who Ward is, what Project Echo is, and what Subject 06 wants with Daniel.

## Production notes

1. **Reuse sets.** The farmhouse living room (night), the front door, the closet and the arm close-up all exist from Part 1. New pieces are the walkie-talkie, the peephole fisheye POV, the cornfield exterior at night, the barricade inserts, the folder close-ups and the floorboard gap.
2. **One rhythm for everything teal.** The walkie LED, the mark, the eyes in the field and the eye under the floor all pulse on the same heartbeat clock as Part 1's radio. The final blink lands exactly on a beat.
3. **The mark keeps its framing.** The arm close-up at 00:18 uses the Part 1 arm setup, so the mark sits in the same spot.
4. **Ward only.** Three short lines, each bracketed by a walkie squelch. The SFX duck under him as in Part 1.
5. **Day stamp.** Only `DAY 10`, on the barricade montage (it's past midnight).
6. **Folder text is diegetic.** SUBJECT 07, TURNED and THIS PROPERTY are printed on the paper, not overlaid, so they read in both 16:9 and 9:16.
7. **Deliverables match Part 1:** a playable animatic plus 16:9 and 9:16 MP4s.
